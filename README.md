# Business Analyst Case Study – Fictional Betting Company

This repository contains my solution to a timed business analyst case study completed in Excel.

## Assignment context
- Format: Excel only
- Time limit: 120 minutes
- Focus: data quality, KPI calculation, customer segmentation, A/B test evaluation, correlation analysis, and business recommendations

## Goal
The purpose of this case study was not only to calculate metrics, but also to interpret the results in a business context and translate them into practical recommendations for management.

## Tasks covered
The case study included:

1. **Data quality check**
   - Identify at least 3 data quality issues
   - Explain how these issues may affect interpretation

2. **Core KPI analysis**
   - Total number of customers
   - Share of verified customers
   - Mean and median of first deposit amount
   - Mean and median of gross revenue over 90 days
   - Share of active customers
   - Top 3 acquisition channels by customer count
   - Top 3 acquisition channels by gross revenue_90d

3. **Customer segmentation**
   - Create at least 3 customer segments based on 90-day behavior
   - Compare segment size, average gross_revenue_90d, average number of bets, and average last_active_days_ago

4. **A/B test evaluation**
   - Compare campaign variants A and B using:
     - open rate
     - click rate
     - deposit rate within 7 days
     - bet rate within 7 days
     - average gross_revenue_7d
     - unsubscribe rate
   - Recommend whether to roll out one variant more broadly

5. **Correlation analysis**
   - Select 2–3 variables
   - Evaluate whether a meaningful relationship exists using correlation, chart, and commentary

6. **Management recommendations**
   - Summarize 3 main insights
   - Propose 2 recommendations
   - Highlight 1 risk or limitation of the analysis

## Deliverables
- Completed Excel file with analysis
- Output sheet with concise management summary

## File included
- `case_study.xlsx` – final Excel solution

## My analytical approach
In this case study, I focused on:
- checking data consistency before interpreting KPIs
- distinguishing between calculation output and business meaning
- comparing average vs. median where outliers could matter
- evaluating A/B test performance using multiple metrics rather than one isolated KPI
- translating findings into practical management recommendations
